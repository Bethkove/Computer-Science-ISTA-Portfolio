# Research Notes

Personal notes on the research papers behind the projects of this portfolio, written in my own words.

## Reading method

Each paper is read in up to four passes:

1. **Overview** (about 10 minutes): title, abstract, introduction, figures and conclusion, to understand the general problem.
2. **Structure** (30 to 60 minutes): precise problem, main idea, method, experimental protocol, main result and limitations.
3. **Details:** the equations that are actually needed to understand the method.
4. **Synthesis:** research question, main idea, method, main result, limitations, and one possible next experiment.
